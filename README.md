# About
Motorcycle Revert is all about driving away from a dangerous fog and not being able to stop

# History
I decided to make a project by myself and I was recently watching JoJo's bizarre adventure Diamond is Unbreakable. I got inspired to make this game after seeing a motorcycle chase which made Josuke destroy and repair their motorcycle to avoid a pedestrian. I wanted to take that idea and turn it into a full game. After some coding and figuring out how to get it to work it was made as an alpha.

# Issues
Sometimes the jump and ground collision breaks, may stick to walls even though you shouldn't, repairing an object your in has issues.
